Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Flixly Shows ("the app") handles information when you use it. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

Information We Collect

Flixly Shows does not collect any personally identifiable information. No account registration is required to use the app. The app accesses publicly available movie and TV show data through third-party APIs.

Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to us:

  • Your watchlist, seen list, and liked titles
  • Your language preference
  • Cached translation results
  • Recent search history

This data remains entirely on your device and is not shared with us or any third party.

Third-Party Services

The app integrates with the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policy:

  • Simkl API — provides movie and TV show metadata
  • TMDB (The Movie Database) — provides streaming provider availability
  • DeepL — translates content descriptions into your chosen language
  • Google AdMob — displays advertisements within the app
  • YouTube — trailers are opened in the YouTube app or browser

Advertising

Flixly Shows displays advertisements via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant advertisements. You can limit ad personalisation through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation

Translations

When you use the app in a non-English language, overview and description text may be sent to the DeepL translation service for translation. Translated results are cached locally on your device to minimise the amount of data sent to DeepL on subsequent visits.

Streaming Provider Data

The "Where to Watch" feature uses TMDB's watch provider data to show you which streaming services carry a title in your region. No personal data is sent to TMDB as part of this feature.

Analytics

Flixly Shows does not use any analytics or crash-reporting services that collect or transmit personal data.

Children's Privacy

Flixly Shows is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact the relevant app store platform.

Data Retention

Since no personal data is collected by us, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Data stored on your device can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage through your device settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by an updated date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are made constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
